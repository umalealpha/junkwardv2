## Overview

The Leads module is a workspace for tracking prospective customers and managing the sales pipeline. This feature is currently under development and being migrated from the backend admin panel; it is not yet available in the frontend portal but will soon enable you to view, manage, and convert leads into policies.

## Who uses this

- Sales agents and managers tracking prospective business
- Team leads coordinating lead distribution and follow-up
- Administrators managing lead sources and conversion tracking
- Anyone with the `lead-list` permission

## Step-by-step

At this time, the Leads module is unavailable. When launched, it will support workflows such as:

- Viewing a list of leads by source, status, and assignment
- Assigning leads to specific agents
- Tracking lead stage progression through the sales pipeline
- Converting qualified leads to quotes and policies
- Analyzing lead sources and conversion rates

Check back soon for full documentation once this feature becomes available.

## Field reference

No fields are currently accessible. When the module launches, the field reference will be updated to include lead tracking fields such as source, assigned agent, contact information, and status.

## Tips & gotchas

- This module is gated by the `lead-list` permission; if you cannot see the Leads menu item, contact your administrator to request access.
- All functionality described here is subject to change during the migration from the legacy backend system.
- Lead data from the backend system will be migrated to this frontend module; details on the transition timeline will be communicated separately.
